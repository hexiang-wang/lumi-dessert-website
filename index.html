It's a pleasure to introduce

Lumi Dessert

Lumi Dessert is a newly opened, San Ramon-based dessert shop specializing in handcrafted, creative tiramisu. Lumi was co-founded by Amber and Elena — two young moms, each raising an adorable daughter. For a long time, we revolved around our families and children, but now, we are excited to build something of our own: Lumi.

Our Story

How Lumi Began

01

A Cozy Afternoon Tea

The story of Lumi began during a cozy afternoon tea. We both love tiramisu, but the traditional tiramisu we bought often has a strong coffee flavor and, in some recipes, contains alcohol — not shareable with our children. This puzzle sparked our thoughts: why couldn't we create a tiramisu for the whole family to share together?

02

From Friends' Kitchens to a Dream

Since that day, we started experimenting with recipes and flavors — making, tasting, adjusting — until we created the tiramisu we were truly excited to share. We began offering it to friends and neighbors. Seeing them enjoy our desserts and share them with their own families gave us the confidence to dream a little bigger.

03

A Small Idea, Shared Widely

We realized that perhaps this small idea could bring joy to many more people. This is what we hope Lumi can become — a tiramisu made not just for us, but for every family looking for a moment to share together.

“To us, dessert is more than something sweet. It can be a part of a birthday, a family gathering, a celebration, or simply a small reward at the end of a busy day. It creates a reason to sit together, share something delicious, enjoy a moment with the people we love, and leave behind a precious memory.”

Our Craft

Handcrafted, Then Handled with Care

Our tiramisu is handcrafted in a certified commercial kitchen in Hayward, kept properly refrigerated, and portioned on-site at the farmers market — so every slice arrives fresh and safe to enjoy.

  • Certified commercial kitchen — every batch made in Hayward, CA
  • Cold chain, respected — refrigerated from kitchen to market table
  • Portioned on-site — cut fresh at the farmers market
  • $11 flat per 8–9 oz serving, every flavor
